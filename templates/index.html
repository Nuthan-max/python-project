    Routing & Dynamic Rerouting Engine

    The core engine implements bidirectional A* search with an admissible great-circle/Euclidean distance heuristic alongside uniform-cost Dijkstra. When an incident or roadblock is placed on an active mission's trajectory, the mission manager detects the disruption and recalculates the optimal path in real-time.
